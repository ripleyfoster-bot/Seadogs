DOUBLE = lift, one quick touch, lift, land: one shot as the thumb lands the second time. A plain lift-and-return does not fire on DOUBLE, so re-gripping is safe.

Dev control, not a purchase. Hands you a fresh ship of the other class — hull, shields and energy at that ship's maxima, hold at its default — and resets pitch, roll and max speed to that ship's row; any override you were flying is dropped. Cargo rides. Every Dray figure is [ASSUMED]. Saves at once, so file on disk below reads the class. Since 100a a fresh hull also undoes a lost commander, as REPAIR does.
These four write to state. The instruments only ever read it — no instrument holds a second copy. These sliders do: a slider position is a copy, refreshed when this panel opens and at no other moment. Since 090a energy and shields recharge — energy +6 a second, each shield +4 a second drawn from energy, shields off below energy 10. All three figures are [ASSUMED], COMBAT §6.1's — seen working on device 3 Oct in 090a, not timed. Drag one down, press FLY and watch the gauge climb. Hull has no slider and never recharges; the ship switch above hands a fresh one. Fuel is moved by a jump and by the market's Fuel service, since 037. This slider buys it for nothing, and that is a dev control, not a price. Loop 89.
Dev control, not a pardon. 0 is Clean, 1 to 9 Offender, 10 and up Fugitive. It writes when you let go, saves at once, and leaves a line in the legal log below. It does not change the ships already here: police that have turned on you stay turned, and the extra police stay, until the next draw — close the app and reopen it, or jump. The slider runs to 30, or to your own figure if that is higher. A jump also takes 1 off (ruling 50). Every legal figure is [ASSUMED]; the two rules are yours — fighting police who came at you first is free (ruling 49), and a record drops by 1 on every jump (ruling 50).
